# Checkpoint Charlie: Privacy Policy

_Effective: November 26, 2026_

Checkpoint Charlie ("the bot") is a private Discord bot. It runs only in the **Bad Country** Discord server, and the server's owner runs it. This policy explains what the bot stores and why.

## What the bot stores
The bot keeps a small file on the computer that hosts it. For each member who interacts with it, the file can hold:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your Discord user ID | To track your checkpoint progress |
| When you accepted the rules | To show you agreed and swore you're 21+ |
| Your intro answers (what to call you, **age you entered**, pronouns/location, "your poison," a weird fact) and the ID of your intro message/thread | To post your intro and let you edit it later |
| If you entered an age under 21: what you typed and when | To keep minors out of a 21+ server |
| When you were verified, and whether you were already a member when the bot was set up | So you get your access back if you leave and rejoin |
| When you last sent a confession | To enforce the cooldown |

The bot also reads your **roles, username, avatar, and Discord account creation date** from Discord while it runs. It does not save these to its file.

## What gets posted where
- **Your intro** is posted publicly in the server's intro channel. It shows "21+ ✔", not your exact age.
- **Confessions** are posted in the confession channel without your name.
- **A private staff-only log channel** inside the server records:
  - joins, verifications, and rule acceptances
  - your stated age
  - a flag if your account is less than 7 days old
  - underage flags
  - **who sent each confession**

## What the bot does NOT do
- It does not read your regular messages or DMs.
- It does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone outside Bad Country's staff.
- It does not use your data for ads, tracking, or AI training.

## How long it's kept
The data is kept until you ask for it to be deleted or the operator wipes the bot's data. Messages posted in Discord (intros, confessions, log entries) stay in Discord until someone deletes them.

## Your choices
- You can edit your intro at any time using the intro button.
- To have your stored data deleted, contact the server owner or staff in Bad Country. Deleting it may mean you have to go through the checkpoint again.

## Discord
Discord processes everything that happens on its platform under its own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
